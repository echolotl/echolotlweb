**Chomb** is an anthropomorphic spider who is covered in dark, ashy crimson fur with small tan stripes all around. He is small comparatively to most of his limited aquantances.
## Overview
Spending a majority (if not all) of his time in one of the many forests around, Chomb doesn't get to interact with many creatures outside of what lives around him. But when he does get to lay one of his many eyes upon a wanderer, he often chooses to play tricks, fool, or steal from them rather than attempt to befriend them or the like. 

As a result of this, most of his time is spent crafting things out (or into) trees, his own webs, or occasionally digging. He is one of the reasons the main path through one of the forests is nicknamed "the Striped seclude", named after the patterns placed into the bark of trees surrounding the trail. 

He loves shiny things, no matter what it is, and keeps various collections of lost items and trinkets shoved into the trunks of some trees. He hates any kind of advancement that confuses him, as he's quite stubborn and stuck to his way of life. Specifically, the various times that [Nautilus](/characters/nautilus) has walked through, Chomb desperately tries to shoo him away or damage him in such a way that he is no longer in the vacinity of "his" trees (mostly because he is afraid of them burning down).
