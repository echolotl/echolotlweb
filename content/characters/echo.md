**Echo** is my pokésona, an anthropomorphic Mimikyu. It was the first character that I had ever made, with [King](/characters/king) coming shortly after.

## Overview
It is a Mimikyu... but a very dog-like Mimikyu at that. Despite its generally normal appearance for what it is, it also has a dog-like tail made of his shadow form dragged behind him. Like an actual Mimikyu, its body is the shadow that is cloaked underneath its actual cloth "skin". 

I imagine its face's lines are perpetually boiling/stirring.

# Inspiration
I don't remember entirely the reason why I wanted to create this character other than I thought it would be cool. Mimikyu was always my favorite Pokémon, and it was basically my profile picture on everything up until I created this thing. You can tell one of my leading reasons to making this thing was to use it as a profile picture, seeing as it was [the second thing I drew of it](/art/echo-pfp).
